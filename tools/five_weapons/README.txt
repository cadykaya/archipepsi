ARCHIPEPSI - FIVE WEAPONS   (revision @REVISION@)

Five experimental weapon types in one isolated range, to compare how
each one feels to fire. Working names, prototype numbers: nothing here
is balanced or final. No enemies. Offline: no bridge, no campaign,
nothing of yours is saved or changed.

START (Windows)
  1. Unzip into one new, empty folder. If it came as two zips (part1of2
     and part2of2), unzip BOTH into that same folder.
  2. Double-click  "1 - START HERE - Five Weapons, Foundry (Windows).bat"
     (On its first run it joins the two parts into the game and checks it.)
     Launchers 2-5 start with the other weapons.
  Or install the zip(s) in the Archipepsi Launcher and pick a mode.
  Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More info, then Run anyway.

THE WEAPONS (keys 1-5 switch at any time)
  1  FOUNDRY      heavy hand cannon. One heavy shot at a time: big kick,
                  big hit. 15 a hit, one shot per 0.72 s.
  2  SIGHTLINE    scout rifle. Quick, accurate, light kick: place hit
                  after hit at range. 8 a hit, one per 0.34 s.
  3  SWITCHBACK   automatic carbine. Hold to fire about 7 rounds a
                  second; it spreads the longer you hold. 3.5 a round.
  4  BULKHEAD     scattergun. Eight pellets in a wide cone: get close,
                  then commit. 4.5 a pellet, one shot per second.
  5  MASS DRIVER  charged kinetic. Hold to charge (1.1 s), release to
                  launch a heavy slug: 10-45 damage by charge, and it
                  shoves loose objects.
  6  Heavy Report on the Static Pulse, exactly as you played it.
  0  The Static Pulse as it ships.

THE RANGE
  A metal dummy, a 40 HP target that falls and stands up again, a gel
  block (organic), a concrete pillar and walls, a steel plate, a timber
  crate, a moving dummy at the back, and two loose bodies by the right
  wall (a 12 kg crate and a 36 kg steel weight) for the Mass Driver.
  Hits leave marks that stay until RESTART.

CONTROLS
  Left click  fire (Switchback: hold; Mass Driver: hold, then release)
  1-5, 6, 0   switch          M   reduced camera motion on/off
  N           placeholder sounds on/off
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look - R back to the mark
  Esc         menu: resume, restart (targets and marks reset), quit

WHAT IS NOT FINISHED
  - Sound: labelled PLACEHOLDERS (old range tones) until Condi's
    SigmAudio renders land. They are level-matched so no weapon wins by
    being louder. N mutes them.
  - Guns: placeholder shapes. Arty's silhouettes and layered Glyph
    effects are pending.
  - Balance: prototype numbers, never tested against real enemies.
