ARCHIPEPSI - FIVE WEAPONS, SECOND PASS   (revision @REVISION@)

Five experimental weapon types in one isolated range, reworked after
your playtest, with Condi's SigmAudio sounds. Working names, prototype
numbers: nothing here is balanced or final. No enemies. Offline: no
bridge, no campaign, nothing of yours is saved or changed.

START (Windows)
  1. Unzip into one new, empty folder. If it came as two zips (part1of2
     and part2of2), unzip BOTH into that same folder.
  2. Double-click  "1 - START HERE - Five Weapons, Foundry (Windows).bat"
     (On its first run it joins the two parts into the game and checks it.)
     The other launchers start with the other weapons (4b: the Sweeper).
  Or install the zip(s) in the Archipepsi Launcher and pick a mode.
  Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More info, then Run anyway.

THE WEAPONS (keys 1-5 switch at any time; 3 and 4 again: the variant)
  1  FOUNDRY      heavy hand cannon. The gun heaves back, the sight
                  settles, the hammer cocks, then the next shot: one per
                  0.72 s, 15 a hit.
  2  SIGHTLINE    scout rifle. The longest reach (60 m) and a hard, quick
                  punch: 12 a hit, one per 0.48 s. Hold RMB to aim.
  3  SWITCHBACK   automatic carbine, its rhythm and spread as you liked
                  them. Hold RMB to aim (tighter, steadier). 3 again:
                  HARD hip-fire (heavier kick, the view climbs).
  4  BULKHEAD     scattergun, two to compare. BREACHER: ten heavy pellets
                  a second, then the pump; point-blank it drops the 40 HP
                  target. 4 again: SWEEPER: seven lighter pellets twice a
                  second, box-fed, no pump.
  5  MASS DRIVER  charged kinetic. Hold to charge (1.2 s), release a heavy
                  slug: 14-60 damage, long reach, little kick; it shoves
                  loose objects. Hold RMB to aim.
  6  Heavy Report on the Static Pulse, exactly as you played it.
  0  The Static Pulse as it ships.

THE RANGE
  A metal dummy, a 40 HP target that falls and stands up again, a gel
  block (organic), a concrete pillar and walls, a steel plate, a timber
  crate, a moving dummy at the back, and two loose bodies by the right
  wall (a 12 kg crate and a 36 kg steel weight).
  TURN ROUND: through the window behind the mark is a long lane, with a
  steel plate and a second 40 HP target at 35 m and a big plate at 55 m.
  Hits leave marks that stay until RESTART.

CONTROLS
  Left click  fire (Switchback: hold; Mass Driver: hold, then release)
  Right click aim down the sights (Sightline, Switchback, Mass Driver)
              V or a mouse side button also aims. B: aim on V only.
  1-5, 6, 0   switch          3 / 4 again   the other variant
  M           reduced camera motion on/off
  N           all weapon sound on/off
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look - R back to the mark
  Esc         menu: resume, restart (targets and marks reset), quit

ABOUT RIGHT CLICK
  In the campaign, right click fires the first Echo slot, where grapple
  and tether Echoes live (a tether swing holds while it is down). Here
  right click aims ONLY while that slot is empty, which it always is in
  this range; if an Echo were there, right click would stay its, and V
  or a side button would aim. Nothing in the campaign was changed.

WHAT IS NOT FINISHED
  - Sound: Condi's SigmAudio candidates, played as she delivered them,
    all at one level. Not approved yet: your ears decide.
  - Guns: placeholder shapes. Arty's models and layered Glyph effects are
    not wired in yet.
  - Balance: prototype numbers, never tested against real enemies.
