ARCHIPEPSI - IMPACT RELAY   (revision @REVISION@)

One room for a hands-on review, built from Dess's D-18 v2 brief: a freight
hall, a lever, a floor plate, a light crate, a heavy weight, a steel
shutter, and a vault behind it. No enemies. Offline: no bridge, no
campaign, nothing of yours is saved or changed.

START (Windows)
  1. Unzip into one new, empty folder. If it came as two zips (part1of2
     and part2of2), unzip BOTH into that same folder.
  2. Double-click  "1 - START HERE - Impact Relay (Windows).bat"
     (On its first run it joins the two parts into the game and checks it.)
  Or install the zip(s) in the Archipepsi Launcher and pick a mode.
  Nothing to install. Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More
  info, then Run anyway.

TWO MODES
  1 - START HERE   The room as designed: the base kit plus the swing
                   tether, which works on any surface, everywhere.
  2 - heavy-hit    The same room, plus the Braided Lash on F: an existing
                   Echo from the game's test campaign that hits for 14.

CONTROLS
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look - left click: Static Pulse
  E      use / pick up / put down
  RMB    swing tether: jump, then hold; let go to drop
  F      Braided Lash (heavy-hit mode only)
  R      back to the start
  Esc    menu: resume, restart the room, quit

WHAT THE THINGS DO (not how to solve the room)
  - The lever powers the floor plate. A powered plate throws whatever
    comes to rest on it, after a short arming, along one arc.
  - The shutter counts how hard it is hit. A blow that is too light does
    nothing to it; heavier blows add up until it gives way.
  - Objects that leave the hall come back where they started.
  Play with the sound on: the machine tells you what it is doing.

WHAT IS REAL HERE
  - Thrown objects are real physics bodies on the engine's own solver.
    They can hit you (harmlessly), miss, be paused in mid-air, and their
    blow is measured from the speed they actually bring.

LIMITATIONS
  - Placeholder looks on the plate, the shutter, the weight and the crate;
    their art is Arty's to come. The lever and the green power line are
    her candidate kit.
  - The Check in the vault and the reward up high are stand-ins: nothing
    is sent anywhere.
  - Existing placeholder sounds only (no new audio).
