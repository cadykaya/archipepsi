ARCHIPEPSI - WEAPON FEEL   (revision @REVISION@)

A firing range for judging how the Static Pulse FEELS to fire. The game's
own weapon, one fixed target 10 m down range, and four firing-feedback
treatments to compare: the baseline the game ships, and three candidates.
No enemies. Offline: no bridge, no campaign, nothing of yours is saved or
changed.

START (Windows)
  1. Unzip into one new, empty folder. If it came as two zips (part1of2
     and part2of2), unzip BOTH into that same folder.
  2. Double-click  "1 - START HERE - Weapon Feel, Baseline (Windows).bat"
     (On its first run it joins the two parts into the game and checks it.)
     The other three launchers start in A, B or C instead.
  Or install the zip(s) in the Archipepsi Launcher and pick a mode.
  Nothing to install. Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More
  info, then Run anyway.

THE FOUR TREATMENTS (keys 1-4 switch at any time)
  1  BASELINE            the Static Pulse exactly as it ships: a small
                         nudge, a quick blue light, a short square blip,
                         the crosshair's own tick on a hit.
  2  A - HEAVY REPORT    a big kick that settles slowly (0.30 s), the view
                         lifts and widens, a warm fireball flash, a low
                         boom, sparks, the target rocks back, a chunky
                         hit marker and a low knock.
  3  B - CRISP SNAP      a short, sharp kick back in 0.08 s, a cold white
                         snap of light, a bright crack, a small puff, a
                         thin hit marker and a bright tick.
  4  C - ECHO RESONANCE  a medium kick, a cyan ring from the barrel and a
                         flash that flares twice, a shot that echoes, an
                         impact ring and a chime that answers a beat (about
                         0.06 s) after the hit.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
  Damage (6 a hit), the rate of fire (one shot per 0.35 s, held or
  tapped), where the shot goes and the target are the same in all four.
  The view's jolt never moves your aim. The counter on screen shows the
  damage, shots and hits, so you can see it for yourself.

CONTROLS
  Left click  fire (hold to repeat)
  1 2 3 4     switch treatment
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look
  E      on the dummy: reset its damage count
  R      back to the firing mark
  Esc    menu: resume, restart the range, quit

LIMITATIONS
  - The target never dies (it is the Echo Lab's dummy), so there is no
    kill confirmation to compare.
  - The sounds are new recipes in the game's existing procedural sound
    bank; no recorded audio. Play with the sound on.
