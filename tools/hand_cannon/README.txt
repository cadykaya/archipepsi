ARCHIPEPSI - HAND-CANNON CANDIDATE   (revision @REVISION@)

Heavy Report (mode 2 of the weapon-feel range) developed into a
hand-cannon candidate, mode H, beside the four weapon-feel treatments as
references. One fixed target, a steel plate, a timber crate and a gel
block to shoot; no enemies. Offline: no bridge, no campaign, nothing of
yours is saved or changed.

START (Windows)
  1. Unzip into one new, empty folder. If it came as two zips (part1of2
     and part2of2), unzip BOTH into that same folder.
  2. Double-click  "1 - START HERE - Hand-Cannon candidate (Windows).bat"
     (On its first run it joins the two parts into the game and checks it.)
     Launchers 2-5 start in one of the references instead.
  Or install the zip(s) in the Archipepsi Launcher and pick a mode.
  Nothing to install. Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More
  info, then Run anyway.

THE MODES (keys 1-5 switch at any time)
  5  H - HAND-CANNON     the candidate:
                         - a heavy, physical recoil that overshoots and
                           settles;
                         - a warm star-and-petal muzzle flash whose light
                           casts shadows, then smoke;
                         - a bullet streak and smoke trail, no white
                           tracer;
                         - impacts by material: sparks and a glowing hole
                           on metal, chips and dust on stone, splinters
                           on wood, mist and droplets on the gel;
                         - a chunky hit marker.
                         C changes its cadence: one shot per 0.35, 0.55
                         (the default) or 0.80 s.
  1  BASELINE            the Static Pulse exactly as it ships (reference)
  2  A - HEAVY REPORT    as you played it (reference)
  3  B - CRISP SNAP      kept as a reference for other weapon types
  4  C - ECHO RESONANCE  kept as a reference for other weapon types

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
  The Static Pulse itself. H only exists in this range; leaving it puts
  the Pulse back exactly. Damage is the Pulse's 6 a hit in every mode;
  H's slower cadences lower its damage per second, and that balance is
  experimental. The view's jolt never moves your aim.

CONTROLS
  Left click  fire (hold to repeat)
  1-5         switch mode           C   H's cadence
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look
  E      on the dummy: reset its damage count
  R      back to the firing mark
  Esc    menu: resume, restart the range, quit

SOUND
  H's sound comes from SigmAudio cues (Condi). The screen says which
  cues are SigmAudio's and which are still placeholders.

LIMITATIONS
  - The hand-cannon model is a primitive placeholder; its art is Arty's.
  - The targets never die, so there is no kill confirmation to compare.
