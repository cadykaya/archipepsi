ARCHIPEPSI - CROSSING D, READABILITY REVIEW BUILD   (revision @REVISION@)

Crossing D wearing Arty's candidate lever and power-line pieces in place of
D's own. Offline: no bridge, no campaign, nothing of yours is saved or
changed.

START (Windows)
  1. Unzip into one new, empty folder. If it came as two zips (part1of2
     and part2of2), unzip BOTH into that same folder.
  2. FIRST, double-click
       "1 - START HERE - Crossing D, NO ENEMIES (Windows).bat"
     The whole Crossing with the Upper Yard empty. (On its first run it
     joins the two parts into the game and checks it.)
  3. Then, for the fight:  "2 - Crossing D, with enemies (Windows).bat"
  Nothing to install. Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More
  info, then Run anyway. Archipepsi-Crossing-D.console.exe is the same
  game with a log window, for when something goes wrong.

CONTROLS
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look - left click: Static Pulse
  E           use / pick up / put down / install
  Right mouse swing tether, in the Courtyard only: jump, then hold it;
              hold W toward where you are swinging; let go to drop
  R           back to your last checkpoint
  Esc         menu: resume, restart the Crossing, quit

WHAT CHANGED FROM D (the look only)
  - Both levers are Arty's floor lever, drawn over D's own colliders:
    stencilled ON / OFF, the handle resting at OFF (right) or ON (left),
    and a pilot on the pedestal that stands up and lights once the lever
    has done its job. The gate lever now faces the yard, where it is
    pulled from.
  - The five power lines are Arty's raceway, laid on the floors and walls
    with turns, corners and saddles, from what feeds them to what they
    power. A line lights only while its source is live.
  - Everything else is D's: rooms, colliders, reach, the puzzle, enemies,
    HP and damage, movement, and the Courtyard-only swing.

LIMITATIONS
  - Candidate art: Arty's kit is still in review, not approved.
  - The raceway does not collide (D's lines did not either): feet pass
    through its 13 cm where it crosses a floor.
  - Its lit stripe is 4 cm wide: clear up close, faint from right across
    the Machine Hall.
  - The ON / OFF status labels and the signs are still D's.
  - The swing tether is still Courtyard-only, unchanged: an open review
    question, not a final rule.

COLOURS   blue: movement - green: power - orange: things that break,
          enemies included

CHECKS are stand-ins: they mark where allocated Checks would go, and they
send nothing.
