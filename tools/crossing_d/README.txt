ARCHIPEPSI - CROSSING D, REVIEW BUILD   (revision @REVISION@)

A comparison build for review: one combined Crossing, four rooms. Offline:
no bridge, no campaign, nothing of yours is saved or changed.

PLAY (Windows)
  1. Unzip into a new, empty folder.
  2. Double-click  Archipepsi-Crossing-D.exe
     That is the whole Crossing, the Upper Yard's fight included.
     "Play Crossing D - no enemies (Windows).bat" is the same four rooms
     with the Upper Yard empty.
  Nothing to install: no Godot, no Python, no first-run import.
  Windows may warn about an unsigned program: More info, then Run anyway.
  (Archipepsi-Crossing-D.console.exe is the same game with a console
  window showing the log, for when something goes wrong.)

CONTROLS
  WASD move - Space jump - mouse look - left click: Static Pulse
  E          use / pick up / put down / install
  Right mouse  swing tether, in the Courtyard only: jump, then hold it;
             hold W toward where you are swinging; let go to drop
  R          back to your last checkpoint
  Esc        menu: resume, restart the Crossing, quit

THE CROSSING
  Central Hall (where you arrive): the exit is at the top of the lift
    tower, and the lift has no power.
  Courtyard (right): launch pads and a spring up to a balcony, swing
    plates for a faster line, an overlook only the swing reaches, and the
    rail back down into the hall.
  Machine Hall (left): a power cell, a plate, a lift-bridge, a lock lever
    and a socket that powers the glass door and the lift.
  Upper Yard (up the lift): one fight, with the exit across it. Running
    past it is allowed.

COLOURS   blue: movement - green: power - orange: things that break,
          enemies included

CHECKS are stand-ins: they mark where allocated Checks would go, and they
send nothing.
