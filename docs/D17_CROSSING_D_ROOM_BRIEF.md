# D-17 — Crossing D: one combined Crossing, room by room

**Dess → Skyiah, Prod and Arty, 2026-10-02. A proposal for review, not
approved for building.** It reconciles Skyiah's A/B/C playtest
(2026-10-01), the earlier Concourse Pier findings and the October 2
handoff with the bridge lane's current contracts. Prod owns
implementation; Arty owns the visual kit.

## The Crossing in one paragraph

Four rooms around one landmark, joined by short, quiet connectors. You
arrive in the **Central Hall** and see the exit at the top of a tower
whose lift has no power. Two wings are open from the start: the
**Courtyard** (movement) and the **Machine Hall** (puzzle). The Machine
Hall's power runs the lift up to the **Upper Yard**, which holds the one
fight and, across it, the exit. Every wing ends with a shortcut back into
the Central Hall, so each return is quicker and visibly changed. There
are three Checks, one in each wing room, and none in the hall.

```
                      UPPER YARD ─────► exit
                     ╱     ▲      ▲
  stair down, opened╱      │lift   ╲ swing gap (optional)
  from the yard side       │        ╲
 MACHINE HALL ──────── CENTRAL HALL ──────── COURTYARD
   glass door back ──►  (lift tower)  ◄── rail back down
```

## Rules for every room

1. **One main activity per room**, a few pieces that support it, and an
   explicit list of what stays out (the cards below).
2. **Tempting, not grabbable.** A Check may be in view early when it
   plainly needs the room's activity: across a gap, high up a wall. No
   Check may be reachable from the room's floor without that activity.
   Prod checks this with the reach measurement already built for the
   gantry (D-9: standable surfaces plus the played jump, 1.40 m). Hiding
   every reward is not the fix.
3. **Clever legal routes stay.** A route that still uses the room's main
   activity is legal and stays, whether in another order, with timing or
   along a movement line. Machine's timed carry still needed all three
   levers. A route that needs none of the activity is a defect to remove,
   such as a hop onto the reward or a ride past the mechanism (the owner's
   Unweighted ruling, 2026-09-25).
4. **The base kit reaches everything Archipelago counts.** Every Check
   and the exit are reachable by walking, jumping, carrying and pulling
   levers, plus the rooms' launch pads, springs, rail and lift.
   - The swing only makes a route faster or reaches local rewards and
     views. It is never the only way to a Check or the exit (§29.5a and
     the DESS-28 ruling).
   - Nothing uses dash.
5. **Plain words.** Signs say where to go or what a thing does: "LIFT —
   NO POWER", "A WEIGHT ON THE PLATE HOLDS THE BRIDGE". Room names are
   plain places.
6. **Color** (the locked vocabulary):
   - blue for movement: pads, springs, swing plates, the rail;
   - green for power: lines, plate lamps, the socket, the lift's badge;
   - orange only for things that break, enemies included;
   - yellow and black for hazards.

   The cell is a mass input, so it stays neutral, as Wisp settled.
7. **What changes stays changed, using records that already exist:**
   latched levers, the installed cell, defeated enemies, discovered
   rooms. In the review build this is session-only, as it was in the
   studies.
8. **Connectors are quiet.** They are short, with nothing to do in them.
   One may hold a window onto the next room.

## Room 3 — Machine Hall (puzzle)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Main activity** | Restore power to the tower lift by understanding one physical idea and then a variation of it. You explore because the window from the hall shows the lift's power line ending at an empty socket across a gap, with a Check on the ledge beyond. |
| **Supporting pieces** | One 18 kg cell in a low wall hatch, reached with E (from B). A floor plate whose green line runs to a lift-bridge over the gap. A lever on the far side whose line runs to the bridge's lock. A power socket on the far side whose line runs on to the glass door and the tower lift. Every line lights when live. The gap is wider than any base-kit jump; Prod measures it. About 22 × 28 m. |
| **Stays out** | Enemies. Movement pieces: no pads, springs or swing plates, so nothing skips the gap. Unlabelled or unconnected controls. Timers. |
| **First view → choices → payoff** | 1) Put the cell on the plate: the bridge lowers while it rests there and rises when you lift it off (a plate holds). 2) Cross and pull the far lever: the bridge locks down for good (a lever keeps). 3) Fetch the cell, carry it over and install it: the socket powers the glass door and the lift. A quick player who carries the cell across before the bridge rises skips step 2. That is legal, so don't block it; the glass door still gets them back. The payoff is the Check on the far ledge, a working lift, and the Central Hall lit up. |
| **Shortcuts** | The glass door from the far side straight into the Central Hall: the door you saw closed when you arrived. |

## Room 4 — Upper Yard (the one fight)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Main activity** | One readable fight, on the way to the exit. You come here because it is the top of the tower you have been looking at, and the exit is across it. |
| **Supporting pieces** | At most 3 enemies, from roles whose current behavior Prod has verified: for example, two ground melee and one ranged on a raised post, its firing lane checked against real projectile volume, not a thin ray. Cover at two heights, a few orange breakable crates that matter as cover, and room to move and recover. Baseline HP and damage. Use ramps for any height the fight depends on, unless melee climbing stairs has been verified. About 24 × 22 m. |
| **Stays out** | Levers and puzzles, random boxes, computers, hazards beyond the marked edge, and enemies anywhere else in the Crossing. Enemies leaving the yard: if the runtime lets them follow you down, the yard's shape keeps them in. No new AI. |
| **First view → choices → payoff** | The lift opens into a sheltered overlook out of the enemies' detection, so you can read the fight before it starts. Go left along cover to the raised post, or right across the low route. The payoff: the yard's Check opens once it is cleared (the existing kill-all objective). The exit door across the yard is reachable without killing everyone, so running is legal. |
| **Shortcuts** | A gate at the top of a stair down to the Central Hall, opened by a lever on the yard side. Returning for a missed Check does not need the lift, and defeated enemies stay defeated, so the yard is calm. |

## How to build it

- **One isolated review build first**, like the A/B/C studies.
  - It lives on a separate branch (for example `wip/crossing-d-review`)
    in its own scene folder. The normal game flow, the bridge and the
    generator stay untouched.
  - Reuse Wisp's study geometry and builders where they fit, and the
    game's existing pieces: Player, the swing tether, launch pads,
    springs, the rail, hand carry, class plates, mounted levers,
    shutters, the bridge actuator, and roster enemies.
- **Two steps.**
  - First build Rooms 1–3, with no enemies, and playtest them.
  - Add the Upper Yard once those play well. The Concourse rubric says
    enemies come after the empty rooms make you want to keep exploring,
    and the Yard is the room with the least evidence behind it.
- **Checks in the review build are stand-ins.** They sit exactly where
  allocated Checks would go, are marked as stand-ins and send nothing,
  as the studies' offline bridge sends nothing. No invented AP items,
  recipients or duplicate grants.
- **Who does what:**
  - **Prod:** layout, runtime wiring, the reach and projectile checks,
    and the documented Windows launch from a fresh folder.
  - **Arty:** the tower landmark, room identity (recognizable from a
    screenshot with the HUD hidden), the color accents, and lever and
    line readability. Reuse the approved textures and models; no new
    kit.
  - **Dess:** check the build against AP, progression and persistence
    before any integration.
  - **Wisp:** hand over the study source and evidence. The branch is
    local, so Prod works from the lean source zip unless Wisp pushes it.

## Conflicts with current work

1. **The bridge says a rail needs dash (DESS-30, new).**
   - The runtime rail is caught by moving along it with the base kit.
     That has been true since `af620d8` (2026-09-02), and A's real-input
     checks show it.
   - The bridge's affordance table still says riding a rail needs dash
     or air dash. That entry predates the rail rework (S9, 2026-08-27).
   - So the generated game only offers rails to campaigns that own a
     dash, which is a hidden dash dependency.
   - The review build is unaffected. The fix is one line plus a test, not
     made because this round allows no production changes.
2. **H-05-ROOM (Dess, 0.5).**
   - It asked for one room carrying all four pillars. The October 2
     guidance gives each room one main activity.
   - This brief reconciles them: the pillars are spread across rooms and
     linked by shared consequences (power and shortcuts), not piled into
     one room.
   - If approved, this replaces H-05-ROOM's single-room demonstration.
     H-05-COMPOSE, the generation work, stays out.
3. **The swing as a featured acquisition.**
   - Integrated, the swing would come from a featured Check. But the
     featured contract (D-02) holds only the gantry's pull grapple,
     `grapple_to_surface`.
   - A swing requirement needs the requirement declared per room, which
     is a small contract change for later.
   - The brief already works without it, because nothing Archipelago
     counts needs the swing.
4. **Blindside.** Its composer, D-6 step 4, is unblocked by Prod's N-18,
   and the bridge lane is holding. Both Crossings showcase grapple and
   machines. Decide whether Blindside's composer goes ahead or waits for
   this playtest.
5. **Capacity.**
   - Prod has open items: the candidate campaign's Zone build failures
     (two of the first twelve still fail, N-20 and HB-F4), the Gear half
     (D-8), and the MENU-INT merge decision.
   - Arty is finishing interface work.
   - This brief competes with both, so the order is yours.

## Pushback: keep it this size

- **Cut first if it grows:** four rooms, three Checks, three shortcuts
  and one fight. Cut in this order: the swing gap to the yard, then the
  overlook's local reward, then the hatch detail.
- **Not in this build:**

    cover crates);
  - GLYPH animation and custom audio;
  - any dash fix;
  - new enemy behaviors or roster changes;
  - new mechanics, Forge or economy;
