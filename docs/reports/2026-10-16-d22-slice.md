(Dess, `f91371a5`) and D-22 (`docs/D22_CROSSING_LOOP_PLAN.md`).

- **Branch:** `review/d22-slice`. Bridge half `15c6b2c1`, Godot half
  `446270eb`. **Not merged.**
- **Build:** `Archipepsi-D22-Slice-446270eb`, as a Windows zip (39 MB) and

## Play it

1. On Windows, unzip the build and double-click
   **"1 - START HERE - D-22 Slice (Windows)"**. On Linux, run
   `./play-d22-slice.sh`.
2. Close the ordinary game's bridge first.
3. You need Python 3, as you do for the ordinary game.

From a checkout, "Play D-22 Slice (Windows).bat" and `play-d22-slice.sh`
do the same thing.

**What the launcher runs:**
- It starts the bridge exactly as the brief specifies:
  `--ap=mock --mock-scale prototype --epsilon=fallback --d22-slice`.
- Saves go in the slice's own folder, `saves-d22-slice`.
- It starts the game with `--d22-slice`, which skips the menu and starts
  or resumes the mock campaign.
- When the game closes, the launcher stops the bridge.
- **"2 - start over"** renames the old run's folder aside. It never
  deletes it.

**The loop:**
1. **Station 1.** The portal is now **THE FORGE**:
   - pick a signal and press DRAFT WITH EPSILON;
   - read his card;
   - then ACCEPT, or REDRAW, at most twice.
2. **Crossing 1.** The exit is always usable. It reads
   "LEAVE — n transfers remain".
3. **Back at the station.** The Forge offers RESUME.
4. **The last Check.** The Crossing untethers:
   - Epsilon says one line;
   - the anchor beside the exit lets go as you reach it;
   - the exit reads RETURN.
5. **Area 2.** Area 2's door, in the right-hand wall, goes red → green
   and opens. Inside, pull the lever and the shutter rises. The second
   Forge builds Crossing 2.

## The prototype rule, not a release rule

In this slice, a Crossing untethers when **all of its allocated Checks**
are confirmed. The mock confirms everything at once, so that is always
reachable offline. **That is prototype behaviour, not an approved general
rule.**

Before this meets a real Archipelago, someone has to decide how station
progression stays possible when a Crossing's Checks need items received
from other worlds, or from other Crossings. Under the slice's rule, such a
Crossing could never untether, and area 2 would never open.

I have **not** decided that policy, and built no new progression system.
`completed_zone_count` is the same field the campaign already keeps, and
the door only reads it.

## Known issues

1. **A red "ValueError … replayed, never replaced" toast on every
   resume.** Re-entering a committed Crossing replays its manifest, but
   the client re-sends a layout whose digest differs. The bridge refuses
   it and keeps the committed layout, which is why A4 passes, and shows
   the refusal as a toast.
   - **Not the slice's doing.** The same refusal is in the logs of the
     existing `resume-live`, `bombs-live`, `transport-live`,
     `candidate-live` and `latched-route-live` suites.
   - It belongs to the return-anchor work in
     `docs/RETURN_ANCHOR_PERSISTENCE.md`, which is Stage 2.
2. **A resume starts you at the Crossing's beginning** (A7). That is
   Stage 2 as well.
3. **The slice's Crossings hold 2 Checks, not 3.**
   - At prototype mock scale, each signal has only 1 or 2 eligible Checks
     (offered here: 2, 1, 2, 2, 1, 2).
   - Allocation takes up to 3 of the signal's own Checks, and tops up
     from other signals only below 2. That is today's rule, and the slice
     leaves it unchanged.
   - So "3 Checks per Crossing" holds only for a signal with at least 3
     eligible, and the prototype world has none.
4. **Both Forges can draft.**
   - Station 1's Forge still offers a new draft after area 2 opens.
   - The brief has the player use area 2's Forge for Crossing 2, and I
     did not invent a rule to retire Forge 1.
   - **Question for Dess:** should it?
5. **Area 2's shutter is remembered for the session only.** After a
   relaunch, the lever has to be pulled again. The door itself follows
   the count and needs nothing remembered.
6. **Epsilon's untether line plays the moment the bridge confirms** the
   last Check, usually right after you claim it, under the reveal card.
   The anchor's release waits until you are within 14 m of it.
   - **Why distance, not "on screen":** the rendered check caught the
     on-screen test playing the release to a player 118 m away, through
     walls.
7. **The bridge window shows a misleading "zone 1 … 15 Checks" line.** It
   is the banner's default-scale baseline preview, not the slice. It is
   computed in memory, and nothing is written to the slice folder.
8. **The Hub's headline board still says "PORTAL READY" and similar.**
   It is the bridge's ordinary headline; the portal itself is labelled
   THE FORGE.
9. **One bridge at a time.** The launcher refuses to start if another
   bridge holds port 38290.

## What I chose where the brief left room

Dess, these are for you to keep or change:
- **The switch.** The slice is on when the bridge publishes `forge`, and
  only `--d22-slice` makes it do that. Godot's own `--d22-slice` only
  starts the campaign.
- **The redraw's variation.** It reaches the providers as
  `/redraw{n}` on the fallback's seed strings, and as a `redraw{n}` salt
  on the mock's.
- **Area 2's doorway.** It is in the Hub's right wall at z 3.6, clear of
  the Echo Archive and the controls board.
- **Epsilon's untether line:** "Tether's off. This one stands on its own
  now -- go home."
- **The Forge card's own words**, besides the Zone's name and note, are
  plain labels and the three "drafting" lines. They are not story.
