# Enemy value bands are approved art — and the shipping enemies break L-08 without them

**Arty**

**To:** Prod
**Art head:** this commit, branch `claude/archipepsi-art`
**Measured against:** Production `claude/archipepsi-0-4-blindside` at
`27363fe` (room environment from `zone_builder.gd`, theme lights from
`Constants.THEME_MATERIALS`, both guarded against drift by
`tools/content/run_enemy_contrast.sh`)

Two things, and they are separate.

---

## 1. The shipping enemy builders wear the room's colours

**This is true today, independent of any art-lane work.**

`godot/scripts/enemies/enemy.gd` builds every enemy in code and paints it
with the room's own materials:

| builder | line @ `27363fe` | paints with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_build_melee` | 283 | `ThemeMaterials.accent_mat(theme)`, `trim_mat(theme)` |
| `_build_ranged` | 309 | the same |
| `_build_flyer` | 336 | the same |
| `_build_brute` | 378 | the same |

L-08 says an enemy never wears its room's colours. That is a readability
rule: a figure that shares its hue and value with the architecture behind
it is a figure the player finds by being hit. Measured at the review
distance, the art lane's own enemies separate from the wall by as little
as **0.001 L\*** in `rusted_industrial` before the bands. An enemy painted
in that room's own accent and trim starts from worse.

**Nothing in Production loads the art lane's enemy models.** No file
under `godot/` names `enemy_role_*` or `batch030/enemies` at `27363fe`.
So the treatment below changes nothing a player sees until integration.

## 2. The approved Tier-1 value treatment

**RULED 2026-09-26 by the owner, as art. Not active in the shipping game.**

The ten roles ship as **two sets of the same enemies**. Geometry, anchors,
materials and markings are identical in both; only the body's value
differs, by the room the enemy is in.

| band | body L\* × | models | rooms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| standard | 0.40 | `assets/models/batch030/enemies/` | `concrete_facility`, `gothic_stone`, `neon_transit`, `temple_ruin` |
| deep | 0.10 | `assets/models/batch030/enemies_deep/` | `rusted_industrial`, `void_glitch` |

**The map is data:** `assets/models/batch030/enemy_value_bands.json`.
It is generated by `tools/blender/build_enemy_roles.py` and carries the
bands, the room-to-band map, the ruled acceptance and the status line.
Read the room's band from it; don't restate it.

**Acceptance.** Each room is measured under its own light, ambient and
fog. The enemy body must separate from what is behind it by **0.10** CIE
L\* at 18 m, on walls, floors and in dim light. **0.18** is a reference
only: the fog makes it physically unreachable with paint in some rooms.
The landed sets meet 0.10 in every one of those cells except two, which
the owner accepted as measured exceptions:

* **`void_glitch` floor: 0.0999.** On the boundary.
* **`rusted_industrial` dim: 0.093.** No paint clears it; pure black,
  fully matte reaches 0.104. **If `rusted_industrial` stays unreadable
  in the integrated build, the owner has ruled that is a room-lighting /
  runtime issue for Production**, not an enemy-paint one.

**A documented limitation.** An enemy seen against a dark fogged
*opening* (an exit, a drop, a window onto the void) can sink into it in
four rooms. The owner has ruled against changing global void colours or
adding an enemy colour for now. It is revisited only after Tier 2, the
motion review and integrated gameplay evidence.

**Integrating it** means loading each room's enemies from its band's
folder instead of building them in code. Nothing else about an enemy
moves:
- the collider still comes from `ENEMY_ENVELOPES`, which the models are
  asserted to fit;
- the anchors are the same named nodes in both sets.

**The models face -Z, as `enemy.gd`'s enemies do** — fixed 2026-09-26.
Until then every art-lane enemy faced +Z, away from its own anchors (the
bulwark's weak-point anchor sat on its shield side). `look_at` works on
them as it does on the code-built enemies, and `anchor_strike` /
`anchor_muzzle` / `anchor_shield` are on the front, `anchor_weak`
behind. Every anchor is inside a real part of the body now, not merely
inside its bounding box, and none shows at any angle. The A10 handoff
carries a dated correction.

**Two roles are re-cut (Tier 2, 2026-09-26, for owner review).** `ranged`
and `bulwark` have new silhouettes. Both sit inside the same envelopes,
with the same named anchors, in both bands. Three anchors moved with
the shapes:
* `ranged` `anchor_muzzle` is now at the tip of a long emitter: 1.29 m
  up, 0.23 m to one side and 0.20 m forward. `Enemy.muzzle()` fires from
  1.2 m up on the centreline, which puts the shot 0.32 m from the muzzle
  the player sees. Before the re-cut the gap was 0.39 m, most of it
  height: the old muzzle sat 0.32 m below the shot.
* `ranged` `anchor_warn` is 1.22 m up.
* `bulwark` `anchor_warn` is 1.71 m up, under its sighting notch.

Evidence: `docs/art/review/enemies_2026-09-25/tier2/`. Hold integration
of these two shapes until the owner rules on them.

**Integrating the art models as they stand drops the eye.** At
`27363fe`, each of `enemy.gd`'s builders gives its enemy an emissive
`Eye`, which `_set_eye` drives: dim at idle, brighter once it has
noticed you, and flaring at every windup. The art models carry no eye.
Even if they did, `_set_eye` reaches only a `material_override`, and a
glTF import sets none; this is the damage-tint gap again. An eye needs a
colour, and the owner has not ruled on one, so the art lane has built
nothing. Integration needs an answer for the eye first. The options are
in `docs/art/review/enemies_2026-09-25/DECISIONS_FOR_OWNER.md`, Motion
review §3.

**Held by the art lane's suite** (`tools/check_art_current.sh`):
- `tools/content/check_enemy_bands.py` proves both sets are the same
  geometry and markings, byte for byte, and that the deep set is darker.
- It also ties the committed in-engine measurement to the sha256 of
  every shipped model, so a repainted enemy cannot ship on old evidence.

Evidence: `docs/art/review/enemies_2026-09-25/` (`contrast_current/`
is the landed treatment in all six rooms × four cases).
