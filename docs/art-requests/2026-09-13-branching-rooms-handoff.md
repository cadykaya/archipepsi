## The one thing to know before you wire them

**`entry` and `exit` keep their names and their meaning.** All three work as
ordinary through-rooms under today's two-socket router — `ALIASES` maps them
to `end_a` / `end_b` and nothing changes. The branches (`branch_east`,
`branch_west`) are **additional**, and a composer that does not assign them
leaves them `SEALED`, which you already close with a slab.

So these can land before the branching composer exists, and get more useful
when it does. No flag day.

## What each socket gives you

Every doorway: a 2.40 × 3.20 m aperture on the wall's **outer face**, a
threshold carrying the floor 0.80 m into the room, an accent **collar**
clearing the opening by 0.30 m on every side, and a named arrival surface.

The collar is sized from `_place_closures` — aperture + 0.6 m, 0.5 m deep —
so a closure, a lock or a return pad seats against something. **All 34
authored doorways in the pack were walked closed as well as open: 102
crossings, and every closed one stopped the body.**

## The stairs

The flight was never short — it has all sixteen treads and reaches 14.00.
`sp_landing_0` was lying across its last three, with 0.38 m of headroom over
`tread14` and `tread15` inside the slab. That is why rays read a 2.62 m step:
they could not see the treads under the landing. The landing now stops at the
flight's west edge.

**This is a manifest change**, contrary to the request — `landing_0` and
`landing_1` are declared Surfaces and their extent was the defect.

### What I need from you

A capsule of your dimensions now completes both routes onto the deck — **16
jumps, one per riser**. It cannot walk them: the measured walk-up limit is
**0.12 m** against a 0.875 m riser, which is your own "separately, and not
yours" note, measured. I left the riser height alone for that reason, and
because a ramp would reintroduce L-95.

So: **the geometry is fixed and the route is completable; whether it is
*playable* is the step-up question, and that is yours.** Please run the
full-player check — my evidence is a capsule, deliberately labelled, with no
movement packages or assistance in it.

## The theme pack is where you asked

`godot/content/theme/` — 37 textures and the descriptor, byte for byte, with
`.import` sidecars generated by running Godot's own importer. Mipmaps on.
**Filter and repeat are not set**: in Godot 4 they are sampler state on the
material, not importer parameters, so there is nothing in a sidecar to set
and the binder sets them. Every descriptor digest is checked against the
exported pixels, so your clause 4 holds before your loader runs.

## For Dess

The three rooms are a vocabulary, not a set: a junction that opens left and
right at the moment you arrive, a junction you can only learn by walking
round it, and a destination that says "this is the end" from its own door.
The destination's space is an `objective` volume and deliberately not a
Check — an encounter, activity, reward or local-key objective all fit, and
baking one campaign's Check into the mesh would make the room useful once.

## Limits, stated

Every route in all three is **flat**, because 0.12 m is the whole budget for
a walking route today. Areas are told apart by enclosure, ceiling and
fittings rather than by height. If the engine gains a step-up, these rooms
gain a dimension — and I would rather add it then than ship a mezzanine
nobody can reach now.
