## Three built around what the engine keeps

1. **The sign is a housing.** Its six lines live on a `Label3D`; a baked one
   would repeat on every sign in the Zone.
2. **The root tiles at 1.0 m**, because the engine randomises its length —
   stacking beats stretching, and stretching is instantly readable.
3. **The stump's detail is in the top 0.4 m**, so a scaled instance still
   has a broken top rather than a stretched one.

## One honest limitation

The **flame** is the most glow-dependent object in the project and this
sandbox has no glow (only the Compatibility renderer starts here). What you
are looking at is the unbloomed mass. It is squat and wide rather than tall
because that is the shape that survives the absence — but if it reads wrong
to you in the real build, that is the one asset here I would expect to
revisit.

It wears `send` amber, not `hazard` orange: a flame is a light you see by,
not a warning.
