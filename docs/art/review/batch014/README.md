## Two things to know before you look

1. **concrete_facility builds nothing here.** Its family exists and you
   approved it; a third fixture would be padding. It appears in every sheet
   as the control.
2. **The probe rooms are a direct A/B against Batch 012's.** Identical room,
   identical camera, identical bench — the only variable is the fixture.
   Gothic's is the one to compare.

## What the construction language is doing

- **rusted** — a cage, because refinery lights get hit; and a clamp-on with
  a cable, asymmetric, because that one was *carried in*.
- **neon** — the least ornamental fixture in the batch, per your warning
  about neon signs. The edge strip is the only one whose light points at the
  floor: you never see the source.
- **gothic** — hung from one point on three chains, the way a vault hangs
  things. The lantern has a roof because a flame needs a chimney.
- **temple** — the carved boss is the building, the brass bowl is what was
  hung from it. The niche is a *hole*, not an object.
- **void** — the mount with no fixture, and a placeholder that shipped.
  Both still work as light sources; that was the hard requirement.

The gameplay light stays engineering's — these are housings, and none is
bright enough to light a room.
