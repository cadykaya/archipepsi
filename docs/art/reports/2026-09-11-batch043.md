# Batch 043 — the status kit, machinery feedback, and the physics-prop family

**Arty**

2026-09-11. Art branch `claude/archipepsi-art`. Design read: Dess's Design 6
at reported revision `a20bf55`.

Three priorities, all delivered as art rather than as an inventory. One new
tooling capability in the Blender exporter, which existed because the owner
asked a question whose honest answer was "the previous kit cannot be driven,
and here is why."

**Everything in this batch is a PROPOSAL.** Nothing is approved, nothing is
bound to runtime, no mechanic is implemented, the twelve shipped shells and
every approved asset are untouched, registry and review states are unchanged,
and the playable build is unchanged. The doorway and Span holds were not
touched: no junction, doorway closure, return device or warp station is in
this batch, and `shell_span_basin` was not opened.

## What was newly authored, adapted, or reused

**Newly authored — the status kit.** 13 status glyphs, 8 compound glyphs, 4
family frames, a compound frame, the player tick, the depletion rule, and the
§33.8 hint in both directions for all eight compounds. 70 editable Glyph
projects, 71 individual transparent PNGs. Nothing was replaced, because the
inventory found nothing to replace: `ASSET_INVENTORY.md` has no status,

**Newly authored — machinery.** The conduit channel and five state bands in
Glyph; three machinery pieces in Blender (`mach_conduit_run`,
`mach_wall_switch`, `mach_receiver_lamp`).

**Newly authored — physics props.** Six of Design 2 §10.1's twelve classes:
`phys_key_component` (8 kg), `phys_power_cell` (40), `phys_mechanical_part`
(55), `phys_girder` (95), `phys_weighted` (140) and `phys_ballast` (320).
The brief named four; the last two were built with the time that remained,
and they were chosen over `CART` and `MOVABLE_COVER` because they **complete
the family's mass ladder**. The question a player asks of one of these
objects is "can I lift that", and the answer is only learnable by
comparison — four rungs left two gaps in the middle of the ladder, six do
not, and §10.3's 60 kg carry line now falls visibly between two of them.

**Reused, not rebuilt.** `shell_corner_left` and its concrete surfaces for
every preview; Batch 042's derelict fields for the dark background;
`prop_crate`, `prop_oil_drum`, `prop_utility_box`, `prop_debris`,
`prop_terminal` and `prop_machinery_unit` as status targets and as the
decorative half of the prop comparison; `tools/artpreview/artbench.gd` as the
runtime GLB loader.

**Adapted:** nothing. Two classes have candidates worth adapting (`PLATE`
from `prop_wall_plate`, `DRUM` from `prop_oil_drum`) and both were left
alone, because the four the brief named were all genuine gaps and adapting a
PENDING asset would have changed something the owner has not reviewed.

## The finding the owner asked for

*"The previous interaction kit's state region survived as a material slot
rather than an addressable node."* Confirmed, measured, at `327c089`:

```
== assets/models/batch028/interaction/int_wall_switch.glb
   nodes 1  meshes 1  materials 3
   node int_wall_switch  mesh int_wall_switch  3 surface(s):
        int_wall_switch_body, int_wall_switch_accent, int_wall_switch_cores
```

All nine primitives are shaped the same way, so the only handle a runtime has
on a Batch 028 state region is `set_surface_override_material(2, mat)`. That
recolours it and does nothing else — it cannot hide, move, scale, rotate, or
independently shade the region, and four of the five conduit states plus the
lever's own position need one of those.

Batch 043's machinery exports every state region as its own named node **and**
its own single material slot, so a runtime gets both handles and picks. The
names and the driving contract are in the machinery manifest. `machinery_2026-09-11/README.md` has the table.

**Batch 028 was not modified.** It is PENDING review and it stays as the
owner last saw it.

## Three things the room corrected that a texture sheet could not

1. **The ballast read as a crate.** First built at 0.86 × 0.66 × 0.66 — near
   enough a cube that in the room it was `prop_crate` in a bigger size. Now
   1.04 × 0.74 × 0.54 with heavier skids and a crown. Weight is proportion
   before it is texture.
2. **A light "bare metal" handling treatment vanished.** `concrete_facility`
   bodies sit at L\* 60–70 and a light steel pad landed on top of them, so a
   16 cm attach pad read as a stain. The handling material is now dark, about
   45 L\* below a painted body.
3. **Four spikes on the KINETIC frame drew a reticle.** §33.10 spends a
   paragraph keeping markers off the crosshair; a marker shaped like one is
   the same mistake in a different hat. Two spikes, on the horizontal axis.

And two the flat sheet corrected before the room ever saw them: the
PERMISSION frame's bars ran to the tile edge and three PERMISSION markers
side by side fused into one rail; and the MATERIAL frame's gaps were so wide
the loop read as three parentheses.

## Faults found in my own pipeline

- **`set_origin` moved a body and left its parts behind.** An addressable
  part authored 2 cm proud of a switch plate exported 7 cm *inside* the
  housing, and the first switch render had no visible indicator in any of its
  three states. Fixed by `common.set_origin_group()`, which takes the anchor
  from the whole asset.
- **I blamed the wrong thing first.** Before finding that, I added a 180°
  rotation in the preview to "make the switch face the room". It did not —
  it turned the lever backwards, and it hid a real fault behind a plausible
  one. The rotation is gone and the comment saying so is in the file.
- **A stray `paint(..., null, ...)` corrupted a grid** and produced a patch
  whose rows were four characters per cell. The authoring script now asserts
  its grid shape before it publishes anything.

## Colour, and the conflict that was not invented around

Design 5 §33.7 wants family carried by *"shape, never colour alone"*; that
permits colour as a second channel and does not supply one. The palette has
no room: six universal colours hold fixed meanings and three of them are held
45° apart by rule. Four more saturated families would collide with one of the
six at any hue.

So the status kit is **neutral** and satisfies §33.7 completely on its own,
and the colour question goes to the owner rather than being decided by the
art lane in the one place the palette explicitly guards. One reserved colour
is used and it is proposed rather than assumed: `send` on the player tick.
The full set of five decisions is in
`docs/art/review/status_2026-09-11/DECISIONS_FOR_OWNER.md`.

## Remaining integration requirements

1. **A HUD.** The persistent tier in every status frame is a mock. §33.10's
   three-tier contract needs a real one before markers can be judged against it.
2. **A world-space marker renderer.** The preview uses screen-fixed
   `Sprite3D`. Nothing in Production draws one.
3. **A status runtime.** Duration, the depletion fraction, the compound
   check and the applied-by-player flag are all preview literals.
4. **A signal graph.** The five conduit states are textures with a driving
   contract and no driver.
5. **Audio.** §19.5's hum, arrival click and rising pitch. `delayed` is the
   one that genuinely suffers without it.
6. **An object contract.** Dimensions and attachment interfaces for the
   twelve classes. The six candidates carry proposed art dimensions and will
   move to fit a real contract.
7. **`ACTOR` status targets** wait on req 31, which still leaves seven of ten
   enemy roles unspawnable. Art did not route around it.
